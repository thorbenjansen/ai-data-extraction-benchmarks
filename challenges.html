Commentary × extraction workflow

Challenges A–D across the selected 50 benchmarks

Each benchmark has at least one challenge assignment and at least one workflow assignment.

Assignments can overlap: a document task may require parsing, retrieval and calculation. Counts across A–D therefore do not sum to 50. A mapping describes task relevance; it does not demonstrate transfer to psychological studies.
