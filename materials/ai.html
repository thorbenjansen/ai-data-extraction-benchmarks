Research context

The starting documents and argument routes follow the author’s current saved manuscript. The agreement–accuracy distinction is explicit. Human, LLM and human–AI workflows share the same accuracy and auditability criteria.

Contents

  • 12 candidate workflow modules, linked to research questions and literature.
  • 8 displayed benchmark cards, 42 additional screened candidates and source data, reference definitions, release-date evidence and descriptive curve inputs.
  • 203 retained source ARAs with their existing evidence and quality labels.
  • Current manuscript bibliography, claim routes, proposed extraction-event schema and a source inventory.

The current eight-family view deliberately selects well-populated positive time trends. It cannot estimate overall progress. The full 50-family evidence remains in the download. Selection criteria and decisions.

Use with an agent

  1. Download and extract the complete package.
  2. Read START_HERE.md, PAPER.md and CURRENT_CONTEXT.json.
  3. Select a workflow module and follow its reference and source-ARA routes.
  4. For benchmark questions, retain the named test condition and cite the result source for each value.
  5. Verify citation-grade statements at the primary source. The retained ARAs are retrieval aids, and their source-grounding limits remain visible.

The package supports research and source retrieval. It is not a validated extraction pipeline or a new performance evaluation. Raw PDFs and the source manuscript are not included.
