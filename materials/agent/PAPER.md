## C01

Evaluate the accuracy of the extraction product and the auditability of the process using the same criteria for human, LLM and human–AI workflows.

Manuscript location: Discussion; Conclusion. Literature: R07, R08, R38.

## C02

The original study compared LLM outputs with existing human-extracted review data. Agreement with that reference does not independently establish the quality of the human judgments or a general accuracy ranking between humans and LLMs. Disagreements require source-based investigation.

Manuscript location: Introduction; human reference data; discussion of Macnamara. Literature: R14, R06, R15.

## C03

Do not assume that LLMs are substantially less accurate than human experts or research assistants on the target task. Compare their performance, errors and complementarities empirically, by workflow step and under documented conditions.

Manuscript location: Human reference data; discussion of Macnamara; Discussion. Literature: R06, R09, R15, R23.

## C04

The 50 selected benchmark families concern component capabilities and related extraction tasks. They do not constitute a psychology-specific extraction benchmark, a pooled estimate of progress, or validation of a complete workflow.

Manuscript location: Lu and Wang; benchmark supplement. Literature: R22, R28.

## C05

Keep model capability, the harness or tools, and the human interaction design distinguishable when comparing workflows. Record which information and assistance became available at each decision.

Manuscript location: Context engineering; human–AI comparisons. Literature: R07, R22, R23, R31.

## C06

Auditability makes it possible to assess and steer the quality of each extraction step. A traceable decision may still be wrong; final accuracy and process auditability therefore remain separate outcomes.

Manuscript location: Discussion; practical reporting; Conclusion. Literature: R07, R08, R38.

## C07

The workflow modules below are candidate components and research questions. Their order, allocation to actors, independence requirements and verification intensity require empirical comparison rather than endorsement of a universally validated workflow.

Manuscript location: Developing a Human–AI Workflow; practical reporting. Literature: R03, R07, R09, R23, R38.

## C08

Protocol reproducibility, human–AI interaction, adjudication and complete audit trails extend beyond the component capabilities represented by Challenges A–D. Missing benchmark coverage remains an evidence gap.

Manuscript location: Coding protocol reproducibility; Discussion. Literature: R22, R23, R35, R36, R37.
