Workflow requirements and benchmark coverage

From extraction steps to observable tasks

The selected 50 benchmarks cover tasks related to Challenges A–D. Later workflow requirements can remain evidence gaps.

Workflow stepQuestionSelected benchmarksLimit
Define variables and coding rulesCan the system apply an explicit extraction schema and its constraints?GSM-Symbolic, BFCL, ComplexBench, DTBench, UNIKIE-BENCH, SUC, Symbolic manipulation, VaxScope, CONSORT diagramsFormal instruction compliance does not validate the construct meaning of a codebook, pilot adequacy or preregistration.
Prepare and read source documentsAre text, digits, formulae, tables and reading order preserved?OmniDocBench, READoc, olmOCR-bench, OCRBench, MDPBench, PureDocBench, Dr. DocBench, DocVQA, MMLongBench-Doc, UNIKIE-BENCH, CONSORT diagramsParsing a page is not a demonstration that an entire article and all supplements were acquired or processed correctly.
Locate relevant evidenceCan the relevant information be found in long or dispersed source material?MultiHiertt, DocMath-Eval, MMLongBench-Doc, LongBench, RULER, HELMET, NoLiMa, InfiniteBench, MRCR, DROP, FRAMES, MuSiQue, HotpotQA, QASPER, SciAssess, LAB-Bench, GAIA, ALCE, RoBBR, TrialReviewBench, Manalyzer, NIAH, LooGLE, MM-NIAH, VaxScopeA retrieval score does not establish that all eligible evidence was inspected or that omissions were eliminated.
Bind values to the correct unitDoes a value refer to the intended study, sample, group, outcome and time point?OmniDocBench, READoc, olmOCR-bench, OCRBench, MDPBench, PureDocBench, Dr. DocBench, DocVQA, ChartQA, ChartQAPro, HiTab, MultiHiertt, TAT-QA, FinQA, DocMath-Eval, MMLongBench-Doc, LongBench, DROP, QASPER, SciAssess, LAB-Bench, TrialReviewBench, Manalyzer, DTBench, UNIKIE-BENCH, TableBench, SUC, MM-NIAH, VaxScope, CONSORT diagramsTable/diagram binding transfers only partly to heterogeneous psychological studies with overlapping samples and outcomes.
Interpret evidence and apply coding rulesCan source evidence be translated into the category required by the codebook?GSM-Symbolic, BFCL, ComplexBench, RoBBR, DTBench, UNIKIE-BENCH, SUC, Symbolic manipulation, VaxScope, CONSORT diagramsGeneric instruction following and biomedical risk-of-bias coding do not establish validity for a psychological review codebook.
Coordinate retrieval, tools and intermediate stepsCan a system select tools and carry a task through several dependent operations?FinQA, LongBench, RULER, HELMET, NoLiMa, InfiniteBench, FRAMES, MuSiQue, HotpotQA, SciAssess, LAB-Bench, CORE-Bench, GAIA, BFCL, ComplexBench, TrialReviewBench, Manalyzer, TableBench, ProcessBench, LooGLEResearch-agent and machine-learning tasks are particularly indirect proxies for data extraction; tools and budgets can differ.
Transform and calculate valuesAre numerical inputs, units and deterministic transformations correct?ChartQA, ChartQAPro, HiTab, MultiHiertt, TAT-QA, FinQA, DocMath-Eval, InfiniteBench, GSM-Symbolic, DROP, Manalyzer, DTBench, TableBench, NUPA, Symbolic manipulation, ProcessBench, LooGLEArithmetic and program execution are not direct validation of effect-size formula selection or study-specific eligibility rules.
Verify source support and detect errorsIs the accepted value or statement supported by the specified source?HELMET, FRAMES, HotpotQA, QASPER, LAB-Bench, CORE-Bench, ALCE, RoBBR, DTBench, ProcessBench, LooGLEDetection and citation metrics differ from generation accuracy and do not independently validate the benchmark reference labels.
Combine human and LLM contributionsHow do human, model and joint performance change with the interaction?No selected benchmark directly evaluates this step.The two interaction benchmarks concern coding or puzzles. No matched comparison of psychological extraction by experts, research assistants and LLMs is established by this register.
Resolve disagreements without yielding to pressureDoes a system accept valid corrections while resisting unsupported pressure?No selected benchmark directly evaluates this step.Sycophancy tasks test model responses. They do not validate source-based adjudication of human–LLM extraction disagreements.
Assess the complete extraction productAre fields and their relationships correct, and is the entire record complete?TrialReviewBench, Manalyzer, DTBench, UNIKIE-BENCH, VaxScope, CONSORT diagramsDirect extraction tasks cover particular documents or domains; none establishes a universal human–AI workflow for psychological evidence synthesis.
Retain an auditable extraction recordCan an independent reader reconstruct evidence, decisions and transformations?Partial diagnostics only: ALCE, DTBench, CONSORT diagramsNo benchmark in this collection validates complete process auditability for the proposed extraction workflow. Citation and structured-output tests assess only components.

The mapping is an authored interpretation of task content. No benchmark in this collection validates the full proposed workflow or complete process auditability.
