Benchmark screening record

Evidence considered for the figures

Eight benchmark families provide the displayed positive time trends. This record documents the 50 candidate families screened using the same data and trend criteria.

The candidate evidence comprises 4,094 source observations in 391 conditions. A sparse or mixed time trend does not establish that a benchmark is irrelevant. BFCL, for example, improves on its overall tool-calling score while adequately sampled subtasks regress, so it is not included in the progress figures.
