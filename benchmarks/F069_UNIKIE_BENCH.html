Commentary challenges and extraction workflow

A · Parsing and understanding semi-structured data

Schema-constrained key information extraction tests document-to-field assignment.

Workflow steps: Define variables and coding rules, Prepare and read source documents, Bind values to the correct unit, Interpret evidence and apply coding rules, Assess the complete extraction product

Mapping basis and transfer limits

Visual business/document KIE; not scientific evidence synthesis by default.
