Commentary challenges and extraction workflow

B · Understanding long contexts

Normalized document-level evidence extraction in systematic reviews provides a close task analogue.

Workflow steps: Define variables and coding rules, Locate relevant evidence, Bind values to the correct unit, Interpret evidence and apply coding rules, Assess the complete extraction product

Mapping basis and transfer limits

Medical review domain; annotation construction and exact-normalization choices limit transfer.
