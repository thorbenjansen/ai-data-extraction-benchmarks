Commentary challenges and extraction workflow

Mapping basis and transfer limits

These links describe the task content. They do not establish successful transfer to psychological evidence synthesis or validate a complete workflow.

  • A: Preserve and interpret document layouts, reading order, text, digits, formulas, tables and figures. Commentary PDF pp. 15–17.
  • C: Process numbers and symbols, apply numerical operations and derive requested values from reported inputs. Commentary PDF pp. 21–23.
  • D: Coordinate multiple intermediate steps, including retrieval, tool use, planning, computation and checking. Commentary PDF pp. 23–25.
