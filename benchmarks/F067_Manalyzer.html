Commentary challenges and extraction workflow

A · Parsing and understanding semi-structured dataB · Understanding long contextsC · Arithmetic and symbolic inductionD · Complex reasoning

Scientific numerical extraction spans text, tables/images and calculation-dependent values.

Workflow steps: Locate relevant evidence, Bind values to the correct unit, Coordinate retrieval, tools and intermediate steps, Transform and calculate values, Assess the complete extraction product

Mapping basis and transfer limits

Author system evaluation; independent benchmark history not established.

These links describe the task content. They do not establish successful transfer to psychological evidence synthesis or validate a complete workflow.

  • A: Preserve and interpret document layouts, reading order, text, digits, formulas, tables and figures. Commentary PDF pp. 15–17.
  • B: Locate and combine relevant evidence across long or dispersed contexts, including distractors and non-literal retrieval. Commentary PDF pp. 18–21.
  • C: Process numbers and symbols, apply numerical operations and derive requested values from reported inputs. Commentary PDF pp. 21–23.
  • D: Coordinate multiple intermediate steps, including retrieval, tool use, planning, computation and checking. Commentary PDF pp. 23–25.
