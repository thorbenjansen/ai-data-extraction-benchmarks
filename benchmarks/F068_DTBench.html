Commentary challenges and extraction workflow

C · Arithmetic and symbolic induction

Schema-based document-to-table extraction tests structured outputs and numerical relationships.

Workflow steps: Define variables and coding rules, Bind values to the correct unit, Interpret evidence and apply coding rules, Transform and calculate values, Verify source support and detect errors, Assess the complete extraction product
