Commentary challenges and extraction workflow

B · Understanding long contexts

Questions and supporting evidence are anchored in complete scientific papers.

Workflow steps: Locate relevant evidence, Bind values to the correct unit, Verify source support and detect errors

Mapping basis and transfer limits

Computer-science papers; paper QA rather than extraction under a meta-analysis codebook.

These links describe the task content. They do not establish successful transfer to psychological evidence synthesis or validate a complete workflow.
